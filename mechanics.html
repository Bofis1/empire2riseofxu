⚔️Combat System

Empire 2 uses real-time point-and-click combat. Left-click attacks toward the mouse cursor, and holding down deals a continuous stream of auto-attacks. All combat is skill-expression — knowing when to commit, when to dodge, and when to parry separates survivors from corpses.

Combo Multiplier

Land hits in quick succession to build a combo. At 3 hits you deal +15% damage, at 6 +30% and at 10 +50% (with a screen shake). Stop hitting for a moment and the combo fades.

🛡️
Block & Off-hand

Hold RMB to block with a shield, or to fire a bow or gun carried in your off-hand. Warriors can ready a parry with the Counter Strike skill.

💨
Dodge Roll

Press SPACE to roll in your movement direction. Brief invincibility frames. Essential for boss phase transitions and AoE avoidance.

⚡
Status Effects

Burn, freeze, poison, stun, bleed — applied by skills and certain weapons. Enemies with active statuses display icons above their health bar.

🎯
Damage Types

Physical, Magic, and Ranged. Each interacts differently with enemy resistances. Mixed builds are viable but specialists deal more to their type.

🧿Skills & Classes

Eight skill slots on hotkeys 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8, plus K to open the full skill loadout screen. Each class has a unique skill tree — pick any combination from your class pool. Skills have cooldowns managed with a mana cost. Some skills scale off ATK, others off INT.

⚔️ Warrior

High physical damage, melee range, survival tools. The front-line fighter. Combo stacking and Berserker Fury are core to the class. Iron Stance roots you in place for its 4 seconds.

🗡️ Rogue

Close-range, high mobility, utility-heavy. 🔰G Levels & Power

Every ten levels is a G. Your G shows beside your level, and gear, portals, the zone banner, the travel map and the world map all carry a G too, so you can tell at a glance what is on your level: the same G as yours is yours to wear, fight and explore. The experience readout counts the levels to your next G, and crossing into one gets its own banner.

🗡️
Gear Has a G

Every piece of gear belongs to a G and can be worn from the first level of that G: G2 gear at level 10, G3 gear at level 20. Reaching a new G opens all of its gear at once.

⚖️
Sized by Slot, G & Rarity

A piece's stats come from its slot, its G and its rarity, so it is worth the same to every class. A higher G beats a lower one of the same rarity: always when they are two G apart, and one G apart unless the older piece rolled far better affixes.

🎲
Affixes Scale Too

An affix gives a share of the piece it is on, so a low-level ring does not carry endgame-sized bonuses. Speed, crit, gems, upgrades and set bonuses are not resized.

💪
Power

One number for how strong your character is, from how hard you hit and how much you can take. It climbs with the whole game: about 5,000 by the end of G1, 100,000 in G5, a million in G8 and a billion in G11. The character sheet shows the typical Power of your G and whether you are BELOW, AVERAGE or ABOVE it. It is a yardstick for gear and builds; combat does not read it.

⬆️
Upgrades at a Glance

Gear tooltips say how much Power a piece would add or cost, bag tiles mark upgrades with an arrow, and the bag can be sorted by Power.

📈
Levelling

A level costs a set number of fights against monsters your own level: 10 in G1, one more for every G after that, and three times as many for the last level of each G, the one that carries you into the next.

⛲
The Outpost Fountain

Stand near the fountain in the XU Outpost plaza and it gives back 5% of your health and mana every half second, empty to full in about ten seconds.

🚫
Why Can't I Wear It?

Clicking gear you cannot wear says why. A skill that can be trained shows a pip on the skill bar, and unspent stat and talent points stay on the status bar.

💎Loot & Crafting

Creatures drop materials, potions and gems from their own tables, and every kill also has a 12% chance at a piece of gear rolled for the zone: from 10 levels below it up to the end of the zone's own G (and never less than 3 levels above it), so every zone drops gear you can put on while you are there. Slain creatures return 5 minutes later. The Wiki bestiary lists every creature's drops and chances.

Bosses — every zone boss, Convergence depth, world boss, rare spawn and Apex Trial boss — drop a full set of their own: a greatsword, a one-hander, a staff, a ranged weapon, a helm, a chest, legs, boots, a ring and an amulet, each with its own model and its own look on your character. A zone boss or world boss kill pays two different pieces and a 45% chance of a third; a rare spawn pays one piece and a 45% chance of a second. Each piece is a weapon 30% of the time, armour 50% and jewellery 20%, and your class's weapon types share 60% of the weapon roll — so the weapons you can wield come up far more often than the ones you can't. An Apex Trial clear pays two pieces of its boss's set (three with two or more modifiers or Cursed) plus gear for the zone you opened it in. The Wiki lists every boss's set and the exact chance of each piece.

Gear that drops in Xeron (Lv 110) is Xeron-Forged: +25% on top of the piece's own stats, shown in its name and tooltip, and it stacks with the piece's affixes. Xeron's creatures hit 15% harder.

Walk over items to collect them. Rarity tiers determine base power and visual appearance:

Gem Socketing — Open inventory (I) and click the gem-socket icon on any equipped item to slot gems into gear. Rubies boost ATK, Sapphires boost INT, Emeralds boost SPD, Topaz boost SPD, Amethysts boost INT+VIT, Void Gems add all stats. Gems come in Small, Medium, and Large sizes.

Crafting — 75 recipes across the Weaponsmith, Armourer, Jeweller and Alchemist benches in the Outpost. Collect zone-specific materials (Crawler Chitin, Wraith Essence, Dragon Scales...) to unlock recipes.

🎒Inventory & Item Bar

The inventory is a draggable, resizable, persistent panel. Open it with I. The default size is 1100×680, capped at 96vw × 90vh, and the layout is saved per character.

🤚
Draggable

Click and hold the header bar to move the panel anywhere on screen. Position is clamped — at least 50px stays visible.

↘️
Resizable

Drag the bottom-right corner handle. Min 560×400, max viewport-bounded. Useful when you want to see the item bar underneath.

💾
Persistent

Position + size saved to localStorage per character. Reopening keeps your layout. RESET button restores defaults.

🔍
Sort & Filter

Sort by Rarity, Type, Name, Newest or Power. Filter by Weapon, Armour, Accessory, Use, Quest. Sell Junk button sells all common items.

Item Bar Bindings

The bottom item bar has 4 consumable slots (Z X C V) for potions, scrolls, food, and other consumables. Two ways to assign:

🖱️
Drag & Drop

Drag a consumable from the inventory bag onto a Z/X/C/V slot. Move the inventory panel out of the way first if it's covering the bar.

⚡
Shift+Right-Click

One-step keyboard shortcut. Shift+RClick a consumable in the inventory and it auto-assigns to the first empty bar slot.

Quick Actions

Right-click in inventory → instant equip. Shift+click → sell. Middle-click → toggle lock (locked items can't be sold or junked). Hover any item for tooltip with stats, its G and the level it opens at, a live comparison vs your equipped piece and the Power it would add or cost. Bag tiles mark upgrades with an arrow. Items on your item bar leave the bag, the way worn gear does.

🌟Ascendancy System

At Level 100, the level cap is reached — but your character is not done. The Ascendancy System unlocks power beyond the cap via Ascendancy Ability Points (AA Points) earned through kills and boss clears. AA abilities split into two categories: universal (any class) and class-specific. Each ability has an AA-point requirement and a point cost to slot.

⚡ As of v92.48, all 9 active abilities are fully functional — including Void Lord's Grasp, Singularity, Ancient Warcry, Salvation, and Titan's Blow. Bind any active to U (or Shift+U for the secondary) for one-press combat use.

Vigg the Wise floats above a turning rune circle just west of the central plaza in the Sanctuary of Aelun and trades Ascendancy Points for things you can hold: mech suit and skycar parts, crafting materials, potions, socket gems, socket punches and lightstones, plus a shelf of gear that turns every day. Only he sells the Starseer line, legendary gear for level 100 in night violet and silver, and the Ascendant line on his elite shelf, a full step above anything that drops at the cap. Hover an item on his shelves for its full stat card and a comparison with what you wear.

∞The Convergence

The endgame zone, unlocked at character Lv 100+. Reality folded after Overseer Zero died, and now every zone of Aethon overlaps inside one 240×240 procedurally generated wound. Each run is a fresh maze of corridors, dead ends and side chambers built from the run's seed, so everyone in your party walks the same one.

Depth Chain

Every depth ends in its own boss: the Depth Sentinel (1), the Depth Inferno (2), the Archon (3), Xerathor (4), the Overseer of Technology (5) and the Forest Ancient (6 and beyond). Kill it, and a magenta portal appears at the kill site. Walk through and you descend — a fresh maze, a new creature mix and harder enemies. There is no bottom.

Each depth has its own creatures, and every one fights with abilities of its own: Depth 1 Broken Reality (Corrupted Xu, Void Marines, Holo Wraiths, Ash Wraiths), Depth 2 Cinder, Depth 3 Storm, Depth 4 Scale & Prism, Depth 5 Machine and Depth 6 Overgrowth. Each creature's abilities are on its card in the Wiki bestiary.

  • Boss HP: 2.8M at Depth 1, +20% per depth to 5.6M at Depth 6 (and beyond). The Vital modifier doubles it.
  • Creatures: +50% HP and damage with every depth, without limit.
  • Boss loot: each depth's boss drops its own full set (see Loot & Crafting).
  • Echo Shard drops: 1-3 per kill plus floor(depth/2). Persists through death.
  • Skip Depth 1 (Archivist upgrade): start every run at Depth 2 instead of 1.
  • Reroll Token (Archivist upgrade): press R within 10 seconds of arriving at a new depth to re-roll all modifiers. 1 per run.

Modifiers (10 normal + 3 cataclysm)

Depth 1 has no modifiers; Depths 2–3 roll one, 4–6 two, and 7 and deeper three. Every 5th depth, a guaranteed cataclysm modifier rolls on top.

  • Burning Ground — passive fire damage at all times.
  • Famine — potions disabled. Reserve Vial upgrade grants 1 charge.
  • Veiled — the minimap is sealed in shadow.
  • Storm Cell — periodic lightning strikes around the player.
  • Bleeding Reality — standing still damages you.
  • Marked — enemies have homing aggro across the zone.
  • Brutal — enemies deal +75% damage.
  • Vital — enemy + boss HP doubled.
  • Density — enemy count tripled.
  • Bounty — enemies drop 3× gold (the "good" modifier).
  • Volatile (cataclysm) — enemies explode on death. 5-tile radius, 2-8% max HP.
  • Frenzied (cataclysm) — enemies attack 60% faster.
  • Hardened Echo (cataclysm) — enemies gain +50% damage reduction.

💎Echo Shards & The Archivist

Convergence-only currency that persists across deaths and runs. Drops from Convergence kills only (no other zone awards shards). Visible in the HUD next to your gold.

Spend at The Archivist, a violet-robed NPC at the central plaza of the Outpost. Press F near him to open his shop. He sells nothing tangible — only upgrades to a 15-node tree.

Tier I — Foundation (50 shards each, all permanent across zones)

  • Vitality I — +5% maximum HP
  • Power I — +5% damage
  • Swiftness I — +5% movement speed
  • Greed I — +10% gold drops
  • Wisdom I — +10% XP gain

Tier II — Convergent (150 shards each, unlocks after 3 T1 owned)

  • Echo Resilience — -10% damage taken in the Convergence
  • Echo Strength — +15% damage dealt in the Convergence
  • Shard Hunter — +50% Echo Shards from Convergence kills
  • Skip Depth 1 — start every run at Depth 2
  • Reserve Vial — +1 potion charge in Convergence (bypasses Famine)

Tier III — Ascendant (500 shards each, unlocks after 3 T2 owned)

  • Boss Slayer — +25% damage to bosses (all zones)
  • Modifier Ward — negate 1 random non-cataclysm modifier per depth
  • Glass Cannon — +30% damage but -15% maximum HP (all zones)
  • Lucky Star — 10% chance enemies drop bonus legendary loot
  • Reroll Token — press R within 10s of depth entry to re-roll modifiers. 1 per run.

Total to max all: 250 + 750 + 2,500 = 3,500 shards. At a typical pace (~25-30 shards per Depth 2 run), that's about 110-140 successful runs to fully clear the tree.

🌀Apex Trials

Kill enough in any zone and a hot-pink Apex portal can open (25 kills in a zone guarantees one); it stays for 4 minutes. The portal asks before it takes you in. Step through into three rooms that end in a boss. Trial creatures and bosses match your level (or the tier's, if that is higher) and fight with their full abilities. The trial's tier follows the zone's level, from Tier I in the Outpost and Patrol up to Tier VIII in the Lv 100+ zones.

8 Tier Difficulty

Each portal is one of 8 tiers. Higher tier = tougher enemies, more stacked modifiers, better loot. Tier scales the dungeon's name, monster levels, drop rates, and the shards you can earn.

  • Tier I–II — Apprentice rifts. Up to one modifier. Iron/Bronze shards.
  • Tier III–IV — Adept rifts. 1–2 modifiers.
  • Tier V–VI — Master rifts. 1–3 modifiers.
  • Tier VII — Sovereign tier. 2–3 modifiers; Mirrored can roll.
  • Tier VIII — XERON tier. 2–3 modifiers; Mirrored can roll. Pays +2 Apex Shards per clear. The ceiling.

Every Apex Trial boss has a full set of its own at its tier's level — The Hollow Initiate (Tier I); Magmaforged Brute (Tier II); The Spore Architect (Tier III); Glaciated Sovereign (Tier IV); Wyrm-Touched Vanguard (Tier V); The Lich Sovereign (Tier VI); The Apex Conqueror (Tier VII); The Apex Conqueror (Ascendant) (Tier VIII). A clear pays two pieces of it (three with two or more modifiers or Cursed), class-weighted like every boss, plus gear for the zone you opened the trial from, gold and Apex Shards. On top of that, the shards buy a unique pool of legendaries that can ONLY be obtained from rifts and the Curator. Highlights: Spore Architect's Staff (T3), Frostfang of the Sovereign (T5), Lich Sovereign's Scepter (T6). The XERON tier (T8) drops with bonus shard quantity — your fastest route to top-end gear.

◈Delves

Every combat zone holds Delves: maze dungeons behind navy blue portals, one in each zone, standing at a random spot. Its navy diamond only shows on the minimap once you have explored that part of the zone, so you have to find it. Walk into one and it asks before taking you in. Each Delve is a fresh maze the size of the Caves of Despair, built in one of ten wall styles matched to its zone, with its own map where the minimap is, filled in as you explore.

🔒
The Sealed Chamber

Kill half of the creatures inside to break the seal on the boss chamber, then beat the boss. Clearing a Delve closes it, and a new one opens in the zone about a minute later.

👾
40 Creatures, 10 Bosses

Four creatures live only in each wall style, each with a signature move, and each Delve draws three of them. Packs wake when you come near and find their way to you through the maze. Ten bosses, one per wall style, each with three moves and three phases.

🎁
5 Delve Sets

Circuitbound Array, the Deepwarden Kit, Sunroot Regalia, Rimegrave Vestments and Starcinder Panoply, with bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces, in every G from 1 to 11. Creatures drop gear of the zone's level with a chance at a set piece (elites far more often); the boss drops two or three set pieces, two more pieces of zone gear, gems and gold.

🚪
Leaving

The green exit portal and the LEAVE DELVE button both ask first and say how much loot is still on the floor: F or Enter leaves, Esc stays. If you leave or fall before the boss is beaten, the portal stays, but the Delve starts over when you come back.

How Spawning Works

  • Timing — The first appears 2–4 minutes after you enter the world, then one every 5 minutes. Each stays for 4 minutes.
  • Random selection — When the timer fires, the game picks one of the seven at random.
  • Group scaling — Its HP grows by 65% for every extra player fighting it.
  • Cross-zone defer — If you're not in the boss's zone, the boss is "pending" — it materializes the moment you arrive.
  • Global announcement — A chat broadcast tells everyone where it spawned. Travel there to engage.
  • Despawn timer — Bosses don't wait forever. Get there fast or miss the window.
  • Manual spawn — Console command spawnWorldBoss(0–6) for testing or community events.

Rewards

Every world boss drops its own full set — all four weapon types and a full set of armour and jewellery — one piece per kill with a 45% chance of a second, leaning toward your class's weapons, plus a tier-appropriate Apex Shard and bonus gold (8,000 + 2,500 per loot tier).

🌐Multiplayer

Empire 2 supports real-time cooperative multiplayer, run entirely through the game server. Up to 4 players per game session. Hosting and joining need an online character.

🏠
Create Game

Host a game from the lobby. Others can see it in the server list and join directly. Optional password protection.

🔁
The Host Can Leave

If the host quits or crashes, the game passes to the next player and carries on — the world keeps running for everyone still in it.

📶
Drop & Rejoin

If your connection blips, you rejoin the same session automatically. Playing solo, your world waits for you for 90 seconds. If the session is gone (the server restarted), the game quietly opens a new one and you keep playing where you stand.

🔗
Persistent Enemies

Every combat zone is live on the server from the moment a game is created. Enter any zone and its creatures are already there. A slain creature returns 5 minutes later. Any player can wake a zone boss, and a slain boss returns after 5 minutes.

📦
Loot, Gold & XP

Loot and gold go to the player who lands the kill. XP is shared with party members in the same zone.

💬
Party Frames

See all party members' HP and MP bars, current zone, and downed status in real time. Trade items via the trade window (Y key, near another player).

✓ Works on any network

Co-op travels over the same secure connection as the rest of the game, so if you can load the game, you can play together — home wifi, mobile hotspots, and work or school networks alike. There's nothing to configure.

✚Co-op: Downed & Revive

In co-op, a lethal hit doesn't kill you outright while a teammate is still standing — you go down instead.

⬇️
Going Down

You drop with 30 seconds to be revived. You can still look around, but can't attack, move or use items. A RELEASE button lets you give up and respawn.

🤝
Reviving

Stand next to a downed teammate and hold F for 3 seconds. They get back up at 40% HP with a moment of invulnerability.

💀
Wipes

If nobody is left standing to help — or you're playing solo — a lethal hit is a normal death. Apex Trials stay solo-style: no downs there.

😇
Resurrection

The Healer's Resurrection skill revives a downed teammate from range at half health.

⚒️Gear Upgrades

Any equippable item can be upgraded from +1 to +10 through the UPGRADE GEAR tab in the crafting menu: the Weaponsmith upgrades weapons, the Armourer helmets, chests, legs, boots and off-hands, and the Jeweller rings and amulets. Each level raises the item's core stats.

✅
Safe Levels

Upgrades from +1 to +4 always succeed.

🎲
Rising Risk

Success chance then falls: +5 90%, +6 80%, +7 70%, +8 60%, +9 50%, +10 40%.

⚠️
Danger Zone

Attempts at +8 and above are risky: a failure knocks the item down one level. Items are never destroyed.

✨
Awakening (+10)

A +10 weapon gains Awakened Strike (a chance on hit to unleash an arcane burst), +10 armour gains Ward (a chance to deflect a hit, up to 20%), and +10 jewellery gains Attunement (lifesteal). Awakened weapons glow, and your character wears an aura.

Bonus to core stats by level: +1 +5%, +2 +10%, +3 +16%, +4 +23%, +5 +31%, +6 +40%, +7 +51%, +8 +64%, +9 +80%, +10 +120%.

🎮Controller Support

Empire 2 supports any Xbox or PlayStation controller (or any Standard-mapped gamepad) via the browser's Web Gamepad API. Plug in, open Settings (⚙), and toggle 🎮 GAMEPAD / CONTROLLER. The setting persists across sessions, and a small 🎮 icon appears in the HUD when active. Mouse and keyboard remain fully functional alongside.

🕹
Twin-Stick Combat

Left stick moves, right stick aims. Combat feels like a top-down shooter. Right stick deflection drives the cursor — all skills and attacks fire in that direction.

🎯
Auto-Aim Forward

No right-stick deflection? The character automatically aims in the direction you're moving. Single-stick play works for casual sessions.

⚙
Skill Cycling

LB / RB cycle through skill slots 1-8. RT fires the selected skill. LT is a quick potion. D-pad maps to item bar slots Z/X/C/V.

🎛
Modal-Aware

B (Cancel) closes the topmost open menu. Combat input is automatically suppressed while panels are open — no accidental swings while reading inventory.

Full bindings reference is in the Settings panel itself, and on the Controls page.
